How the journeys work

Puerto Rico is small enough to cross in a day and large enough to fill a week. A journey joins our days in an order that keeps the road kind: the old city first, then the mountains, then the coasts.

Every day begins with pickup where you are staying, and every table and entrance is held before you arrive.

Coast, mountains, south and west. Puerto Rico, end to end.

For travelers who want the island whole. Five days that begin in the old city, follow the coast east, cross the mountains to the south, and end on the western shore as the light goes down.
